Privacy Policy

Revive · Last updated 4 November 2026

Revive is a personal wellbeing app for nutrition, sleep, training and health tracking. It is built to keep your information private by default.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — meals, sleep, weight, check-ins, training sessions, medical notes and the private symptom log — is stored on your device. Without an account, nothing is sent anywhere.

Optional account and sync

If you choose to sign in (email code or Google), your app data is stored in a private database so it can sync between your devices. It is linked to your account, used only to provide sync, protected by row-level access rules so only you can read it, and transmitted over encrypted connections. Your app lock and PIN never leave the device.

Food search

When you search for a food online, the search words are sent to Open Food Facts to look up nutrition values. No personal data is included.

What we never do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No use of your health information for anything other than showing it back to you.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Your choices

  • Export your data at any time: Me → Settings → Export file.
  • Delete local data: Me → Settings → Delete all data.
  • Delete your account and synced data: Me → Account → Delete my account.

Health information

Revive offers general wellbeing guidance and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always follow the advice of a qualified clinician. In an emergency in mainland China call 120.

Children

Revive is intended for adults (18+).
